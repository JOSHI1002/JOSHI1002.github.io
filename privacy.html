Privacy Policy for CVAT-Rclone

Updated November 12, 2026.

CVAT-Rclone is a personal-use Google Drive integration for a locally managed CVAT research annotation environment.

Google data accessed

With permission from the Google account holder, Rclone uses read-only Google Drive access to discover folders and files, inspect relevant metadata, and retrieve selected file contents for annotation.

Purpose and storage

Authorized data is used to browse and import research images into the operator's locally managed CVAT system. Selected files may be processed or cached locally. OAuth credentials and tokens are maintained in operator-controlled configuration storage; this informational website does not receive or store those tokens.

Sharing

The operator does not sell Google user data or provide it to advertising networks. Google Drive, Rclone, CVAT, and the operator's local infrastructure process data as necessary for this authorized workflow. This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which handles website access under GitHub's own privacy terms.

Access, revocation, and deletion

The account holder can revoke the OAuth authorization through Google Account settings. The operator can remove locally stored Rclone tokens and imported or cached images. Revocation prevents future authorized retrieval but does not automatically remove local copies already imported into CVAT.

Contact

For inquiries about this integration, contact the operator through the GitHub profile.
