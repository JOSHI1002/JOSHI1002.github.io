CVAT-Rclone

Personal Google Drive integration for a locally managed CVAT research workflow.

CVAT-Rclone lets the operator authorize read-only access to files in their own Google Drive so rock-core images can be browsed and imported into a self-hosted CVAT annotation workspace.

This informational website is not a public CVAT server, an image-sharing service, or a sign-up portal. Google account access requires authorization by the account holder.

The site does not display rock-core images, research annotations, OAuth tokens, or other private research data.
